Decision

Consider a limited follow-up pilot after confirming margins, costs and stock. Synthetic evidence cannot establish an actual promotion's value.

Confidence intervals describe estimation uncertainty under the model. They are not rollout prediction intervals. A nonsignificant placebo does not prove no spillovers.
